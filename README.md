### 🧩 Who we are

**BADBRAIN Studios** is a narrative technology studio that builds the systems, platforms, and digital experiences where stories are made, shared, and lived. We design **narrative infrastructure**—the tools, flows, and interfaces that shape how audiences encounter culture in the next era.

Grounded in Columbus, Ohio, we work at the intersection of storytelling, systems thinking, and digital experience.

---

### 🧩 What we do

We help organizations and creators build cleaner, faster, and more meaningful digital experiences:

- **Narrative strategy & creative direction**  
  Clarify your story, audience, and architecture before writing a single line of code.
- **Web & platform design**  
  Design and implement systems that scale with your narrative, not just your traffic.
- **Copywriting & content systems**  
  Craft copy that thinks, structures content that flexes, and designs that breathe.
- **UX, SEO, and digital experience development**  
  Experience‑driven builds that respect both accessibility and performance.
- **Proprietary tools & story‑driven products**  
  From custom WordPress plugins to experimental narrative tools, we build the infrastructure stories run on.

---

### 🧩 What we believe

Our work is guided by a small set of core principles:

- **Narrative First**  
  The story always leads the system, not the other way around.
- **Systems Thinking**  
  We design for flows, not just files.
- **Cultural Rigor**  
  Our work is context‑aware, historically grounded, and culturally fluent.
- **Builder Mentality**  
  We ship, iterate, and rebuild; we don’t just plan.
- **Radical Clarity**  
  Complexity lives in the code; not in the user’s experience.
- **Independent Studio Spirit**  
  We own our voice, our tools, and our constraints.

---

### 🧩 How we work

We partner with organizations, founders, and teams that want:

- Clearer narrative strategy.  
- Stronger, more resilient systems.  
- Digital experiences with cultural weight and technical precision.  

Our work is collaborative, iterative, and built around long‑term partnerships, not one‑off campaigns.
